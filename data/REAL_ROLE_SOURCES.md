## Collection and pairing

Unavailable or redirected pages were replaced before freezing. The first fifteen sources use profiles in odd ordinal positions; the last fifteen use the even positions. All thirty synthetic profiles had appeared in prior fictional-role tests. The new pairings were fixed before this phase, without outcome-based changes. Paraphrasing introduces selection and wording bias. Full local snapshots are retained privately for source checking; public data provides short attributed summaries.
