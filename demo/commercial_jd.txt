Commercial Product Manager, Local Services Subscription (fictional demonstration)

A fictional marketplace wants a product manager to improve a paid subscription for local service providers. The PM will interview customers and providers, decide which plan benefits solve a real problem, and set pricing and packaging with finance. They will map the free-to-paid conversion funnel, design onboarding and retention experiments, and define success measures for activation, renewal, refunds, and customer trust. The PM will partner with marketing and sales on go-to-market positioning and with engineering on delivery. This is a standalone synthetic demo, not a live vacancy or part of the blinded evaluation set.
